Pay at a convenience store, or by bank (Pay-easy at an ATM, or internet banking; not at a bank counter)."

faq:
  - q: "How much is the fee?"
    a: "For applications received on or after October 1, 2026, it depends on the period of stay you are granted. At the counter: 3 months or less ¥10,000; over 3 months up to 6 months ¥18,000; over 6 months but less than 1 year ¥25,000; 1 year ¥33,000; over 1 year but less than 3 years ¥48,000; 3 years or more but less than 5 years ¥64,000; 5 years or more ¥75,000. Online: ¥10,000, ¥15,000, ¥21,000, ¥27,000, ¥42,000, ¥56,000 and ¥65,000 for the same periods, plus a payment fee of ¥330 (periods under 3 years) or ¥550 (3 years or more). If you receive a specified residence card (特定在留カード), an extra fee may be added. Applications received by September 30, 2026 pay the old fee (¥6,000 at the counter, ¥5,500 online) with revenue stamps, even if they were made online."
  - q: "Can the fee be reduced?"
    a: "The fee may be reduced if you are in financial hardship comparable to people who need public assistance (生活保護) and need humanitarian consideration. A reduced fee is ¥10,000 whatever period you are granted. Reductions are not available for online applications, so apply at the counter and ask the immigration office."
  - q: "My period of stay ends before I get the result. Can I stay?"
    a: "If you applied before your period of stay ended, you can stay with your current status until the decision is made, or until 2 months after the end date, whichever comes first. Do not wait until the last day."
  - q: "I got an email asking me to pay the fee. Is it real?"
    a: "If you applied at the counter, the fee is shown on a postcard and can only be paid with revenue stamps. For online applications, the email with the payment link comes from an address ending in @service-dgft.com, and the emails sent when you choose a payment method and when payment is completed come from @veritrans.jp. The Immigration Services Agency says that any other email asking you to pay this fee is a scam, and that it never asks online applicants to pay by credit card or QR code or sends them a paper payment notice."
