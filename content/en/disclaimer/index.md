## Disclaimer

- This site is **not a government website** and has no affiliation with the Tokyo
  Metropolitan Government, the ward offices, or any public body.
- This site provides **navigation, not legal advice**. Procedures, requirements and
  opening hours can change at any time. **Always confirm with the official source** —
  if anything on this site differs from an official page, the official page is correct.
- We are not responsible for the content of external websites, including the official
  websites we link to.
- "Last verified" means a human checked that the link worked and the information matched
  the official source **on that date** — not that it is still correct today.

## Link policy

We link only to:

1. **Official government domains** — city and ward sites (lg.jp), national ministries
   and agencies (go.jp), and metro.tokyo.lg.jp. These are always preferred.
2. **Public institutions** — official English pages of quasi-governmental bodies
   (for example, the Tokyo Intercultural Portal Site run by the Tokyo Metropolitan
   Foundation TSUNAGARI).
3. **Essential service providers** — for utilities only (electricity, gas), where the
   provider is a private company and no government page can start the service for you.

We never link to aggregator sites, affiliate sites, or advertising-supported guides.
Every link is verified by a human before it is published; anything we could not verify
is not published.

## Privacy {#privacy}

- **No ads, no tracking.** This site has no advertising, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no cookies.
- **Your ward choice.** If you pick your ward, the choice is saved only in your own browser (localStorage) so the
  page can show it next time. It is never sent to us.
- **Delivery.** The site is served by Cloudflare. As part of delivering pages, Cloudflare processes connection
  data such as your IP address ([Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)).
- **MCP server for AI tools** (`https://mcp.official.tokyo.jp/mcp`):
  - Your IP address is used only to count requests per minute, to prevent overuse.
  - Cloudflare keeps a log of each request (time, URL and connection data such as the IP address) for up to
    7 days. We use it only to check that the service works and to stop abuse.
  - Our code does not write the content of your requests (your questions or tool arguments) to the logs.
    There are no accounts and no cookies.
- **npm package.** `npx -y @dror-jp/official-tokyo-mcp` runs on your own computer and only downloads this
  site's public data files.
- **If you contact us.** Email to [fix@official.tokyo.jp](mailto:fix@official.tokyo.jp) is used only to
  check your report and, where possible, reply. Issues on GitHub are public under GitHub's terms.
- We do not sell or share personal information with anyone.
- Operator: DroR Corporation ([dror.co.jp](https://dror.co.jp)).
